How to Use DibaTech Resume Templates

Calm, step-by-step guidance to help you download, edit, and finalize your resume with confidence.

1. How to Download a Resume Template

  • Select a resume design you like from the Home page.
  • Under the template, click the Download PDF button.
  • Your browser will download the file to your default downloads folder.
  • Open the PDF to quickly review the layout and structure.

2. How to Edit in Canva

  • On your chosen template, click the Edit on Canva button.
  • You will be redirected to Canva (log in or create a free account if needed).
  • Once the template opens, click Use this template or similar.
  • Wait a few seconds for Canva to load your editable copy.

3. How to Edit in Other Editors

  • Click the Edit on Other Editors button for your chosen template.
  • You may be redirected to tools such as Google Docs, Notion, or other editors.
  • Make a personal copy of the document, if the editor requests it.
  • Start editing directly in your preferred tool.

4. How to Personalize Your Resume

  • Replace the name at the top with your full name.
  • Update contact details: email, phone number, city, and relevant links.
  • Carefully replace each section: experience, education, skills, and summary.
  • Keep bullet points clear and focused on achievements, not just tasks.
  • Remove any sections that are not relevant to your profile.

5. How to Export Your Final PDF

  • After editing, review your resume slowly from top to bottom.
  • In Canva or your editor, choose Download or Export.
  • Select PDF (Print) or a high-quality PDF option when available.
  • Save the file with a clear name, such as Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf.
  • Open the exported PDF to make sure everything looks clean and aligned.

6. Recommendations for Different Roles

  • Corporate & Business Roles: Choose layouts that are traditional, structured, and easy to scan.
  • Tech & Product Roles: Use clean designs with clear skills and project sections.
  • Creative Roles: Select templates with a touch of personality but keep content professional.
  • Academic & Research Roles: Prioritize education, publications, and research experience.

7. Tips for a Clean, Professional Resume

  • Keep your resume to one or two pages, depending on experience.
  • Use consistent dates, fonts, and spacing throughout.
  • Avoid heavy colors, distracting icons, or dense paragraphs.
  • Focus on clarity: your resume should be easy to read in under 30 seconds.
  • Ask a trusted friend or mentor to review it before sending to employers.
