Follow these simple steps to customize your resume

1

Download the Resume

Click the "Download PDF" button above to save the resume template to your device.

2

Go to Canva

Click "Open Canva" below to launch Canva in a new tab. Sign in to your Canva account or create a free account if you don't have one.

3

Upload & Edit

In Canva, click on "Uploads" in the left sidebar, then drag and drop the downloaded PDF. Click on it to open and start editing with Canva's powerful tools.

💡 Pro Tip

Canva will convert the PDF into an editable format. You can then customize colors, fonts, text, and layout to match your personal brand!
